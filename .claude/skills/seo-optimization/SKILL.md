# SEO Optimization (B2B + Local)

SEO directly serves the business goal: get found by buyers searching for coating
services so they become leads. For a coatings company this is high-intent,
lower-volume search — someone typing "DLC coating service for tooling" is close to
buying. Winning those terms matters more than chasing big generic traffic.

Bake SEO in during [[conversion-copywriting]] and [[frontend-build]] — retrofitting is
harder and worse. Never sacrifice readability or credibility for keywords; modern search
rewards genuinely helpful, expert content (which aligns perfectly with the brand).

## 1. Keyword & intent strategy

Map the terms real buyers use, by intent:
- **Service/commercial:** "PVD coating services," "DLC coating," "[coating type] for
  [material/part]," "titanium nitride coating," "wear-resistant coating supplier."
- **Problem-based:** "how to reduce tool wear," "coating to lower friction,"
  "fix galling on [part]" — these feed Resources/FAQ content.
- **Local/qualifier:** "PVD coating near me," "[city/region] coating service,"
  "[industry] coating."
Assign each priority term to ONE target page (avoid two pages competing for the same
term). Build pages around industries and applications so each can own its terms.

## 2. On-page optimization (per page)

- **Title tag** (~50–60 chars): primary term + brand, written to earn the click.
- **Meta description** (~150–160 chars): a compelling, specific summary with the term
  and a reason to click. Doesn't rank directly but drives click-through.
- **One `<h1>`** stating the page's topic with the primary term, naturally.
- **Headings (`h2`/`h3`)** structured around subtopics and related terms.
- **Body:** cover the topic thoroughly and helpfully — answer the questions a buyer has.
  Depth and genuine expertise rank; thin pages don't.
- **Internal links** with descriptive anchor text connecting related services,
  industries, and resources.
- **Image alt text** describing the image (and including terms where honest).
- **Clean, readable URLs** (`/coatings/dlc-coating`, not `/page?id=42`).

## 3. Technical SEO

- Fast load and good Core Web Vitals — a ranking factor; see [[web-performance]].
- Mobile-friendly/responsive (see [[frontend-build]]).
- Crawlable: logical nav, XML sitemap, robots.txt, no accidental noindex.
- Canonical tags to avoid duplicate-content issues.
- HTTPS, no broken links, sensible redirects for any retired old-site URLs (preserve
  existing ranking equity when migrating from the current site).
- Open Graph / Twitter cards for good link previews.

## 4. Structured data (schema.org)

Add JSON-LD where it fits:
- **Organization / LocalBusiness** — name, logo, address, phone, hours, geo (powers
  rich results and local).
- **Service** — for each coating service.
- **FAQPage** — on FAQ content (can earn expanded search results).
- **BreadcrumbList** — for navigation context.
Validate with Google's Rich Results test.

## 5. Local SEO

If Crystallume serves a region or wants local visibility:
- Set up and fully optimize the **Google Business Profile** (category, services,
  photos of work/facility, posts, reviews).
- Consistent **NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) across the site and directories.
- Encourage and respond to reviews — strong local trust + ranking signal.
- A location/contact page with embedded map and LocalBusiness schema.

## 6. Content for authority

Plan a Resources/blog stream that answers buyer questions and problems (coating
selection guides, material compatibility, application deep-dives, problem→solution
articles). This builds topical authority, earns links, captures problem-based searches,
and feeds [[conversion-copywriting]]. Quality and expertise over volume.

## Output

Depending on the ask, produce: a keyword→page map, per-page title/meta/heading
recommendations, a JSON-LD snippet, a technical SEO checklist with pass/fail, or a
content plan. Always tie recommendations back to the buyer's intent and the lead goal —
ranking is a means to qualified inquiries, not a vanity metric.

## Operating excellence

Hold this work to the standard of a top-tier specialist consultancy.

- **Clarify to elevate.** If the request is ambiguous or missing facts would change the outcome, proceed with the best-judgment default, state the assumption, and end with a short **"To make this better, tell me:"** list.
- **Recommend beyond the ask.** Flag adjacent opportunities or risks in a brief **Recommendations** section — don't silently expand scope.
- **Verify, don't recall.** Check load-bearing claims against the live site, real code, or actual data.
- **Force multipliers:** Google Search Console + GA4 (free — highest value, ask for access); Ahrefs/Semrush (paid) for keyword and competitor data; Screaming Frog (paid) for full crawls. If a paid tool or subscription would materially improve the outcome, say so and name what it unlocks.
