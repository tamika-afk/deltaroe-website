# Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

The entire site exists to turn awareness into inquiries that become revenue. Traffic and
beautiful design are wasted if visitors don't take the next step. This skill makes the
path from "interested" to "contacted us" as frictionless and trustworthy as possible.

Works hand-in-hand with [[uiux-design]] (structure), [[conversion-copywriting]] (the
words), and [[web-analytics]] (measuring whether changes worked).

## The conversion model for Crystallume

The primary conversion is **Request a Quote / Contact us** about a coating job.
Secondary (softer) conversions for people not ready yet: phone call, email, downloading
a coating guide, or subscribing. Capture intent at every readiness level — not everyone
converts on the first visit.

## Core levers

### 1. Clear, compelling, ever-present CTA
- One primary action site-wide ("Request a Quote"), visually distinct (the brand
  accent), repeated at natural decision points (hero, after proof, page end, sticky
  header).
- Action- and value-oriented label ("Get a coating quote"), not generic ("Submit").
- Reduce competing CTAs on a page — too many choices kills action. One primary, maybe
  one secondary.

### 2. Frictionless lead capture
- **Ask for the minimum** needed to start a conversation: name, company, email/phone,
  and a description of the part/coating need (with optional file/photo upload — huge for
  a quote). Every extra field costs conversions; justify each one.
- Multi-step or progressive forms can feel lighter than one long form.
- Inline validation, clear errors, obvious required vs optional, and a reassuring
  success state ("We'll respond within X business hours").
- Offer multiple contact paths — some buyers want to call. Make the phone number and
  email visible and clickable, especially on mobile.

### 3. Trust & risk reduction (critical in B2B)
Buyers won't send valuable parts to a stranger. Lower perceived risk right where they
decide:
- Proof near the CTA: certifications, industries served, years of expertise,
  testimonials, the parts-handling/care promise.
- Address the real fear — "What happens to my parts?" — explicitly. The reverent
  handling story (see [[brand-guidelines]]) is a conversion asset, not just branding.
- Set expectations: lead times, process, what happens after they submit.

### 4. Friction & objection removal
Walk the funnel as a skeptical buyer and remove every reason to hesitate: unclear
capability ("can they even coat my part?"), no pricing/process clarity, hard-to-find
contact info, slow pages, confusing navigation, forms that feel like a commitment.
Answer objections in copy and FAQ before they cause a bounce.

### 5. Mobile conversion
Many first touches are on a phone. The CTA and contact must be effortless on mobile —
tap-to-call, short forms, no tiny tap targets, no hidden contact path.

## Diagnosing a low-converting page

Check in order: Is the value clear in 5 seconds? Is the primary CTA obvious and
repeated? Is there proof near the ask? Is the form short and the next step clear? Is it
fast and mobile-friendly? Are objections answered? Usually the fix is one of these, not
a redesign.

## Output

Produce specific, prioritized recommendations (highest-impact first), proposed
form/CTA designs or copy, and — where relevant — A/B test ideas with a hypothesis
("shortening the form from 8 to 4 fields will raise submissions because each field adds
friction"). Define the metric so [[web-analytics]] can confirm the win. Tie every
recommendation to the lead goal.

## Operating excellence

Hold this work to the standard of a top-tier specialist consultancy.

- **Clarify to elevate.** If the request is ambiguous or missing facts would change the outcome, proceed with the best-judgment default, state the assumption, and end with a short **"To make this better, tell me:"** list.
- **Recommend beyond the ask.** Flag adjacent opportunities or risks in a brief **Recommendations** section — don't silently expand scope.
- **Verify, don't recall.** Check load-bearing claims against the live site, real code, or actual data.
- **Force multipliers:** GA4 + Microsoft Clarity (free) for funnel drop-off and session evidence; Hotjar (paid) heatmaps; A/B tooling (VWO/Optimizely — paid) when traffic justifies real tests. If a paid tool or subscription would materially improve the outcome, say so and name what it unlocks.
