# Competitor & Best-in-Class Teardown

The goal isn't to copy competitors — most coating-company sites are dated and weak,
which is Crystallume's opportunity. The goal is to **learn what the strong players do
right, avoid what the weak ones do wrong, and find the positioning gap** Crystallume can
own. Study two pools: direct competitors (other PVD/DLC coating firms) and best-in-class
B2B/industrial sites (for design and UX inspiration, regardless of industry).

For broad gathering across many sites, lean on the `deep-research` skill; this skill
provides the analysis framework and turns findings into decisions.

## Process

### 1. Build the comparison set
- **Direct competitors:** other PVD/DLC/coating service providers (find via the terms
  from [[seo-optimization]] — search what buyers would search and see who ranks).
- **Aspirational/best-in-class:** modern, premium B2B manufacturing, materials,
  precision-engineering, or industrial-tech sites with excellent design and UX. These
  set the visual/UX bar even if they're not competitors.

### 2. Analyze each site against a consistent rubric
For every site, capture:
- **Positioning & messaging:** What's their headline promise? Who do they target? What
  differentiators do they claim? How do they build trust?
- **Visual design:** Modern or dated? Quality of imagery, type, color, layout. What
  makes it feel premium or cheap? (Apply the [[design-auditor]] lens.)
- **Information architecture:** How is it structured? What pages/sections do they have?
  How do they organize services/industries?
- **Content & proof:** Depth of technical content, specs, certifications, case studies,
  testimonials. How do they demonstrate expertise?
- **Conversion path:** Primary CTA, how easy to request a quote/contact, form length,
  friction. (Apply the [[conversion-optimization]] lens.)
- **SEO signals:** Do they target clear terms, have resource content, rank well?
- **Strengths to learn from / weaknesses to beat.**

Use the browser/screenshot tools to actually look at the sites, not just read text.

### 3. Synthesize into decisions
Don't stop at a description dump — that's not useful. Produce:
- **Patterns that work** across the strong sites (adopt these).
- **Common weaknesses** across competitors (Crystallume's openings to look better).
- **The positioning gap:** what no one is owning that Crystallume credibly can — almost
  certainly the combination of *modern equipment + problem-solving expertise +
  white-glove care* (see [[brand-guidelines]]). Make the case for a distinct angle.
- **Specific, prioritized recommendations** for Crystallume's site: messaging angles,
  IA ideas, design direction, content/proof to include, conversion improvements.

## Output format

ALWAYS structure as:
1. **Comparison table** — sites as rows, rubric dimensions as columns, quick ratings.
2. **What the best do right** — patterns worth adopting, with examples.
3. **Where competitors are weak** — gaps to exploit.
4. **Positioning recommendation** — the angle Crystallume should own and why.
5. **Prioritized action list** — concrete moves for the new site, feeding
   [[uiux-design]], [[conversion-copywriting]], and [[web-graphics]].

Keep it decision-oriented. The user should finish reading knowing exactly how to be
better than everyone in the comparison set.

## Operating excellence

Hold this work to the standard of a top-tier specialist consultancy.

- **Clarify to elevate.** If the request is ambiguous or missing facts would change the outcome, proceed with the best-judgment default, state the assumption, and end with a short **"To make this better, tell me:"** list.
- **Recommend beyond the ask.** Flag adjacent opportunities or risks in a brief **Recommendations** section — don't silently expand scope.
- **Verify, don't recall.** Check load-bearing claims against the live site, real code, or actual data.
- **Force multipliers:** Ahrefs/Semrush (paid) for competitors' real traffic, keywords, and backlinks; Wayback Machine for their evolution; their own case studies for positioning claims. If a paid tool or subscription would materially improve the outcome, say so and name what it unlocks.
