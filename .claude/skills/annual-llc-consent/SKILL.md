# Annual LLC Consent and Record-Book Session

An LLC's liability shield is defended with records: a written consent each year, a ledger
that matches reality, and evidence the company was run separately from its owner. The trap
is treating the operating agreement as the finish line. Done means: a signed consent for
the year, a completed separateness checklist with any failure fixed, an updated register,
and the year's financial statements filed behind Tab 10.

## Inputs
- The record book (`PTAI-Company-Record-Book-<date>.html` source, or the equivalent for
  another company): Tabs 4 through 10 are the templates this skill fills.
- Year-end figures from the bookkeeping system: revenue, net income, distributions to the
  member, capital account balance, any owner loans either direction.
- Current facts: registered agent, principal office, insurance carrier and limits, Statement
  of Information status, franchise-tax payment confirmation, list of contracts and licenses
  in force, domains and their renewal dates, trademark status.
- Any decisions taken in the year that need recording: S-corp election, new products,
  hires, loans, capital contributions, changes to the operating agreement.

## Process

### 1. Gather the numbers from the books, not from memory
- Export the profit-and-loss and balance sheet for the calendar year. Record revenue, net
  income, distributions, and the ending capital account. If the bookkeeping is not
  reconciled through December 31, reconcile first; a consent built on unreconciled books is
  a liability, not a record.

### 2. Fill the annual consent (Tab 7)
- Complete every bracket: year, the four figures, agent and office, Statement of Information
  status and next due date, franchise-tax plan, insurance in force, other decisions.
- Attach the financial statements as the exhibit. Date it in January; the owner signs it.

### 3. Complete the separateness checklist (Tab 8)
- Walk every row against evidence: the bank export (no personal charges), a sample of
  invoices and contracts (full LLC name and "Managing Member" signature), the address and
  email in use, the bank balance against three months of expenses plus refund exposure,
  reconciliation dates, filed consents, current filings and insurance, any owner loans
  documented in writing, and the client-data policy.
- Any row that fails is fixed before the checklist is marked complete, and the fix is noted
  (for example, "one personal charge on the company card, reimbursed 1/12, receipt filed").

### 4. Refresh the registers (Tabs 4, 5, 9)
- Register of members and managers: confirm no change, or record the change with its date.
- Membership ledger: confirm the certificate and units; record any transfer or new issuance.
- Contracts and licenses: list every client agreement, partner license, insurance policy,
  domain, agent service, mail service, and trademark application or registration with its
  term, expiry and status. Remove nothing; mark ended items as ended.

### 5. Confirm the filings (Tab 3 and Tab 10)
- Statement of Information: filed date and next due date match the Secretary of State
  record (check bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov Business Search).
- Franchise tax: last payment confirmation number; next April 15 on the calendar.
- Form 568 and the personal return: preparer engaged, due dates on the calendar.
- File the year's statements, the bank year-end statement, and the tax return behind Tab 10.

### 6. Produce the packet
- Render the completed consent, checklist and registers as one dated PDF
  (`PTAI-Annual-Consent-<year>.pdf`), copy to the three deliverable locations, and tell the
  owner to sign, then place the signed copy in the physical or scanned record book.

## Output format
ALWAYS structure as:
1. **Year summary** — revenue, net income, distributions, capital account, with the source
   export named.
2. **Annual consent** — the completed document, ready to sign.
3. **Separateness checklist** — each row with pass or the fix applied.
4. **Registers** — the updated member, ledger, and contracts tables.
5. **Filings confirmed** — Statement of Information, franchise tax, returns, with dates.
6. **Open items** — anything that could not be completed and what it needs.

## Guardrails
- Figures come from the reconciled books; never estimate a year-end number for a consent.
- The owner signs; the skill prepares. Never represent a consent as adopted before it is
  signed and dated.
- Never delete a register row; the book is add-only.
- Personal financial information of the owner (SSN, personal accounts) never appears in the
  record book.
- Company records stay in the company's own folder, never in another company's repository.

## Operating excellence

Hold this work to the standard of a top-tier specialist consultancy.

- **Clarify to elevate.** If the request is ambiguous or missing facts would change the outcome, proceed with the best-judgment default, state the assumption, and end with a short **"To make this better, tell me:"** list.
- **Recommend beyond the ask.** Flag adjacent opportunities or risks in a brief **Recommendations** section — don't silently expand scope.
- **Verify, don't recall.** Check load-bearing claims against the books, the Secretary of State record, or actual files.
- **Force multipliers:** the bookkeeping system's year-end reports; the Secretary of State Business Search (free) for the filing record; a CPA's year-end review before the consent is signed. If a paid tool or subscription would materially improve the outcome, say so and name what it unlocks.
