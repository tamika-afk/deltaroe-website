How this works — the one-minute version

Your website was built so that a person with zero technical background can run it. Here's the whole secret:

  1. Your website's "recipe" (all its pages, words, prices, and the Roe chatbot's brain) lives in one place online called GitHub — think of it as the master document, with a complete history of every change ever made.
  2. A service called Vercel reads that recipe and serves the live website to the world, automatically, within about a minute of any change.
  3. Claude is an AI assistant that reads and edits the recipe for you. You tell it what you want in plain English — "raise the sound bath price to $130" — and it makes the change everywhere it needs to go, shows you, and publishes it. It already knows every rule and detail of your site, because we wrote all of that knowledge into the project itself.
Nothing you or Claude do can permanently break the site. Every change is saved in history and can be undone, and if a change has an error in it, the live site simply stays on the previous version until it's fixed.

This guide has three parts: A — set up your accounts (about an hour, one time), B — transfer day (Mike hands you the keys), C — daily life with Claude (the fun part).

Part A · Set up your three accounts (one time, ~1 hour)

You'll create three free-to-start accounts. Use the same email for all three (we suggest tamika@deltaroe.com) and store each password somewhere safe — a password manager or a notebook that stays home. Never share passwords with anyone — including Claude. It will never ask for one; if anything ever does, stop.

A1 · GitHub (where your website's recipe lives)

  1. Go to github.com → click Sign up.
  2. Enter your email, create a password, pick a username (e.g. tamika-deltaroe).
  3. Verify your email when GitHub sends the confirmation. The free plan is all you need.

A2 · Claude (your AI web assistant)

  1. Go to claude.ai → Sign up with the same email.
  2. Subscribe to the Claude Pro plan (about $20/month — this is your "web person" salary now, and it also gives you Claude for everyday business use: writing emails, social posts, flyers, anything).
  3. That's it. There is nothing to install. Claude works right in your web browser (and the Claude phone app works too).

A3 · Vercel (the service that keeps the site online)

  1. Go to vercel.com → Sign up → choose "Continue with GitHub" and log in with your new GitHub account. (This links the two — that's the point.)
  2. Stop there for now — the website gets connected on transfer day, below.

Part C · Daily life with Claude — your new web person

C1 · Opening the workshop

  1. Go to claude.ai/code in your browser (bookmark it).
  2. First visit only: it will ask to connect to GitHub — click Install the Claude GitHub App and approve. This is how Claude reaches your website's recipe. If it shows an environment confirmation screen, accept the default.
  3. In the repository picker, choose deltaroe-website.
  4. Type what you want, in plain English, and press Enter. That's the whole job.

C2 · How a change goes live (what you'll see)

  1. You describe the change. Claude edits the recipe in a safe workspace in the cloud and shows you what it changed.
  2. You click Create PR (a "pull request" — think: a change slip waiting for the owner's signature).
  3. On the GitHub page that opens, click Merge. That's your signature. Vercel notices, rebuilds, and the change is live in about a minute.
  4. Not sure about a change? Just don't merge it — the live site is untouched. Merged something you regret? Tell Claude "undo my last change" and it will.

  • Usage limits: Claude Pro allows a generous number of messages per 5-hour window. If you ever hit it, it just asks you to come back in a few hours — nothing is lost. For a few website tweaks a week you will rarely notice.

Part D · The rules your website lives by

These are already built into the project (Claude enforces them) — listed here so you know your own house rules:

  • Imagery: no stock photos of faces, ever — nothing that misrepresents you. Hands-only stock is fine. The best future upgrade is a real photo shoot of you in the studio.
  • Voice: warm, direct, empowering — your voice. Visitors are addressed gender-neutrally ("friend"), because your clients include men. Your book titles (FLY Queen) always stay as titles.
  • Colors: midnight black + antique gold + champagne. Chakra colors appear only where they mean something (the chakra page, The Clearing, Sound of Paint).
  • Honesty: everything is "complementary wellness," never a substitute for medical care. No medical claims, ever.
  • Prices update everywhere at once — pages, FAQ, Roe, and the summary that AI search engines read. Claude handles this automatically.
  • Roe can never go rogue: she only says what's been written for her, and she costs $0 to run.

Part E · Launch checklist (from Wix to your new site)

In order, whenever you're ready — ask Claude to help with every step:

  1. Square Appointments: set up your services, prices, deposits, gift cards, and memberships in Square (Mike's Owner's Manual §2 covers daily use). Then tell Claude: "Square is ready — switch all the Book buttons from Wix to my Square booking page: [paste your Square booking link]."
  2. Save anything you love from the old Wix site — it disappears at cutover. (Ask Claude to help capture it.)
  3. Point deltaroe.com at Vercel: in Vercel → your project → Settings → Domains → add deltaroe.com; it shows you exactly what to change where your domain is managed. Claude can walk you through it click by click.
  4. Google: update your Google Business Profile website link; finish GBP verification; click "Ask for reviews" there and give Claude the review link — it wires up the site's review funnel. Ask Claude to set up Google Analytics + Search Console (both free).
  5. Cancel Wix. 🎉
