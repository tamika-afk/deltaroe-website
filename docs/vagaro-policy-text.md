# Vagaro policy text — paste-ready

Keep this in sync with `/policies` on the website (`app/policies/page.tsx`), the FAQ
(`lib/faqs.ts`), the Roe chat answer (`lib/roe-kb.ts`) and the intake consent checkbox
(`app/intake/IntakeForm.tsx`). If a term changes in one place it must change in all five,
or the studio is promising two different things.

Where this goes in Vagaro: **Settings → Business Settings → Policies** (cancellation
policy), and the same text can be pasted into the booking-confirmation email template so
clients receive it at the moment they book.

⚠️ Vagaro can only *charge* a late-cancellation or no-show fee automatically if card
capture is switched on, which needs Vagaro's card processing approved. Until then the
policy still stands but is enforced by hand — Tamika has to ask for it. Do not assume it
is being collected automatically.

## Short version — for the booking screen / policy field

```
Cancellation & Refund Policy

Delta Roe is one person holding one room. When you book, that hour is held for
you and no one else.

• Reschedule or cancel free with 24 hours' notice.
• Less than 24 hours' notice: 50% of the session price.
• No-show (no arrival, no contact): 100% of the session price.
• Arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean we can't run the session, and is
  treated as a late cancellation. Sessions always end at their scheduled time.

Rescheduling is always preferred to cancelling — the week you feel too busy for
your session is usually the week you need it most.

Refunds are issued as studio credit, which never expires and can be used for any
service, membership or gift card. If the studio cancels, you choose: full refund
or credit.

Memberships renew monthly and can be cancelled any time, with no fee and no
notice period. Cancelling stops the next payment; the month already paid stays
yours to use.

Gift cards never expire and carry no fees.

Life is not tidy and this is not a trap. Genuine emergencies, sudden illness and
family crises are handled by a human being, not a rule — call (916) 206-1752.

Full policy: https://deltaroe.com/policies
```

## Notes on the legal bits — do not "tidy" these away

- **Gift cards (California Civil Code 1749.5):** it is unlawful to sell a gift card in
  California with an expiration date, or to charge service, dormancy or inactivity fees.
  Balances under the statutory threshold are redeemable in cash; that threshold rose from
  $10 to **$15** in April 2026. Never add an expiry or a fee.
- **Memberships (California Automatic Renewal Law, amended 1 July 2025):** if a member
  can subscribe online, they must be able to **cancel online, at will, without
  obstruction** — a "call us to cancel" gate is not lawful. Signup needs affirmative
  consent (no pre-ticked boxes), a retainable confirmation of the terms, and annual
  renewal reminders.
- This is drafted from the studio's own terms and current California consumer law, but
  it has **not been reviewed by a lawyer**. Worth an hour of one's time before the
  Apothecary starts taking real money.
