# Shopify refund policy — The Apothecary

Written 28 August 2026 to Tamika's three decisions: **30-day window**,
**customer pays return shipping**, **no returns on opened soap**.

**Where this goes:** Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Refund policy.
It could not be set through the API — the Shopify connector lacks the
`write_legal_policies` scope, which Shopify gates deliberately.

**Why it matters beyond good manners:** Google Merchant Center requires a
return and refund policy and suspends accounts without one. Until this is
saved in Shopify, connecting Merchant Center will fail review.

⚠️ This is a practical business policy, not legal advice. Worth having the
studio's accountant or attorney glance at it, particularly for California's
rules on posted refund policies.

⚠️ **Open question deliberately left open:** this says *soap* only, exactly as
instructed. Candles and loose-leaf tea are also consumables with the same
hygiene argument, and they are not covered. Decide whether to extend it before
the first opened-candle return arrives, not after.

---

We want you to love what arrives. If something isn't right, here is exactly how it works.

## You have 30 days

From the day your order arrives, you have 30 days to request a return. Items need to come back unused, in their original packaging, and in resalable condition.

## Return shipping is yours to cover

We don't provide prepaid return labels, and original shipping charges aren't refunded. We recommend using a tracked service — we can only refund items that actually reach us.

## Opened soap can't be returned

For hygiene reasons, soap cannot be returned once it has been unwrapped or used. Unopened bars are returnable as normal.

## If it arrives damaged or wrong, that's on us

Email us a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will replace the item or refund you in full, including shipping. You won't pay anything to send it back. This applies to opened soap too — if a bar arrives broken, we'll make it right.

## How to start a return

Email Info@deltaroe.com with your order number and what you'd like to return. We'll confirm the return address before you ship anything, so please wait to hear from us rather than sending items back on your own.

## Refunds

Once your return arrives and we've checked it over, we'll refund your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take a few days longer to show it.

## Digital items

Downloadable products are non-refundable once the download has been accessed.

## Sessions and appointments

This policy covers physical goods from The Apothecary. Reiki sessions, sound baths, coaching and other studio appointments are covered by our separate cancellation policy at deltaroe.com/policies.
